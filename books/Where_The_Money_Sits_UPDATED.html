3. Four steps

  1. Fake move — past a drawn line, ~$2–10, back inside, long tail
  2. Bigger pile check — if yes, stand down
  3. Turn — body close past last peak/dip
  4. Pullback — gap / last opposite candle / broken line

Stop past fake extreme. Target = next real pile (red/green dashed cash-out lines).
